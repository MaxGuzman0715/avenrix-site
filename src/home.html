      FAQ

      Questions people ask first.

      Is it free to join?

      Yes. Creating a profile and getting matched costs nothing.

      What kind of work is on Avenrix?

      Part-time, project-based and seasonal work across seven fields: tech and data, retail and sales, customer support, operations and admin, creative and content, logistics and field work, and specialist roles.

      How many hours do I need to offer?

      As many as you actually have. Some roles need a few hours a week, others need full shifts. You set your availability, and we only match you with work that fits it.

      Do I need a résumé?

      No. Describe what you do and what you've done in plain words. You can add documents later if a role asks for them.

      How do I get paid?

      The rate and schedule are agreed before any work begins. Payment goes through Avenrix according to that agreement.

      Where is the work?

      Remote roles are open to most locations. In-person roles depend on where clients are hiring, and your location helps us show you what's nearby.
