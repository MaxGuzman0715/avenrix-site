FAQ

Questions businesses ask.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short call we send a written proposal with a fixed price or an hourly rate, so you know the cost before you commit.

Who actually does the work?

The Avenrix team. You'll know who is working on your project, and you'll have one point of contact throughout.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Ownership of the work we create for you transfers to you under the contract.

Can we sign an NDA first?

Yes. We're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share anything confidential.

How do you keep us updated?

Weekly updates by default, plus whatever tools your team already uses, such as Slack, email or your project tracker.
